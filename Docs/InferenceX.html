Root-cause diagnosis of every pain point

All causes directly observed in server logs and `bench.py` runs
1. `nanbeige` 17.1× prefill collapse (`8.7` vs `148.7 tok/s`)
Fixed

Asymmetric `cache-q8` (`--cache-type-k q8_0 --cache-type-v q5_1`) on `nanbeige` (`head_dim=128`, 22 full layers) caused Metal Flash Attention to miss its fused fast path. Even at `d=2048` in `llama-bench`, `q8_0/q5_1` timed out after 240 s while symmetric `q8_0/q8_0` ran 176.9 / 12.53 tok/s.

Fix appliedChanged `cache-q8` macro to symmetric `--cache-type-k q8_0 --cache-type-v q8_0` (`204.7` 4K / `148.7` 16K prefill), and `q4_0/q4_0` for 4-slot swarm.
2. `gemma4a` screen glitching & memory exhaustion
Solved

`gemma4a` holds `14.40 GiB` weights + `1.02 GiB` mmproj + 25 SWA layers (`8` KV heads each) + 5 full layers (`head_dim=512`). Combined with `--cache-ram 4048` and Docker Desktop's `13.0 GiB` VM reservation, residency exceeded `18.62 GiB` and starved `WindowServer`.

Fix applied`--cache-ram 256 --ctx-checkpoints 4`, `-c 49152`, symmetric `q4_0` KV → **16.21 GiB** peak, `31.96 tok/s`, zero glitches.
3. Startup >60s (`mcp.json` + warmup + swap thrash)
8.3× faster

`llama-server` logs proved `--mcp-servers-config` spawns 4 Docker stdio containers (`DuckDuckGo`, `Thinking`, `Youtube`, `Wikipedia` = 29 tools) on every swap (`2.0 s` warm, much longer cold) while skipping 6 HTTP/malformed entries (`Web Fetch` lacked `"command"`). Warmup + 4 GiB `--cache-ram` + swap pressure pushed cold swaps past 60 s.

Measured`xspark` direct boot: `6.36 s` (agent + warmup + 4G cram) → **`0.77 s`** (`--no-warmup`, no MCP, 512M cram). `qwen3a` swap load: `23.2 s` → **`6.5 s`**.
4. Parallel agentic swarm (`tiny` / `smol`) & thread tuning
Up to 4.26×

`--parallel 1` serialized concurrent swarm calls (`c2 ≈ c1`). Switching to `--parallel 4 --kv-unified` scaled `xspark` to **`65.48 tok/s`** (`5.22 GiB`). On `nanbeige` at `c=124000`, `q8_0` 4-slot thrashed (`7.05` c1 / `21.28` c4), whereas `q4_0` 4-slot hit **`42.59 tok/s`** (`10.63 GiB`). `-tb 8` vs `-tb 4` landed inside ±0.5% noise.

Fix appliedKeep `-t 4 -tb 4` (4 P-cores); use `${4-slot}` on `xspark` (`q8_0`) and `nanbeige` (`q4_0`, or `q8_0` + `2-slot`).

Speculative decoding: math vs measured reality on Apple M4

Direct test of `qwen3a` embedded MTP (`blk.40.nextn.*`) & `ngram-simple`

Why 80.2% acceptance ran 2.59× slower

`qwen-3.6-35B-A3B-heretic-IQ3M.gguf` includes an embedded MTP draft layer (`blk.40`, `366 MB` with `346 MB` of its own MoE experts). With `--spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 1`, `llama-server` achieved 80.2% draft acceptance (`170/212`, mean length `1.80`), yet throughput fell from 36.93 tok/s to 14.28 tok/s.

Every speculative step doubles routed MoE expert union reads (`3.13% → 6.15%`), reads `blk.40` + `0.42 GB` `output.weight`, and forces GDN state save/rollback across 41 layers — raising step time from 27.08 ms to 126.5 ms/step (`210` vs `384` graph reuses).
