Apple M4 local inference hill-climb

Controlled benchmarks on localhost:11111 across 81 runs (Nov 17 fleet optimization + Nov 23 Inkling-2-Mini-IQ4XS, live omp request telemetry, and validated llama-swap-optimised@latest.yaml.

What this report answers: Single-token generation on Apple M4 (24 GB) is memory-bandwidth bound at 93.2 GB/s (any operation under 38.6 FLOP/byte is limited by how fast weights move from RAM, not GPU compute). Meanwhile, macOS reserves a hard Metal GPU limit of 18,186 MiB (MTL0); crossing ~16,500 MiB starves WindowServer and causes screen flickering. Root-cause diagnosis of every pain point

Every failure observed in server logs, omp telemetry, and bench.py traced to its underlying cause and verified fix.

1. inkling screen flickering & Metal OOM at -ub ≥ 512
Solved

inkling2_loop allocates Metal compute scratch proportional to ub × n_ctx: 833 MiB at -ub 128 jumps to 3,339 MiB at -ub 512 and 6,680 MiB at -ub 1024, breaching 18,186 MiB and throwing kIOGPUCommandBufferCallbackErrorOutOfMemory (starving WindowServer).

Fix appliedLocked 2-ram strictly to -ub 128 --cache-ram 256 (15,713 MiB ≤ 16,500 MiB), while giving gemma4a explicit -ub 512 --cache-ram 256.
2. inkling 75.3 s avg TTFT (shortconv wipes + ling swaps)
13.6× rollback

shortconv.l_cache=4 blocks partial sequence trimming; default -cms 8192 wiped 99.6% of a 2.8K-tok branch (27.44 s, 10 cached). Simultaneously, omp routed side-channel tasks to ling, evicting inkling from llama-swap.

Fix applied--ctx-checkpoints 16 --checkpoint-min-step 2048 (2.01 s, 2,692 cached), added ink to mutually exclusive moe set, and route side-channel work off ling.
3. nanbeige 17.1× prefill collapse (8.7 vs 148.7 tok/s)
Fixed

Asymmetric cache-q8 (q8_0/q5_1) on nanbeige (22 full layers) caused Metal Flash Attention to miss its fused fast path. Symmetric q8_0/q8_0 recovered 16K prefill to 148.7 tok/s; q4_0/q4_0 hit 42.59 tok/s in 4-slot swarm.

Fix appliedSymmetric cache-q8 (q8_0/q8_0) globally and ${cache-q4} ${4-slot} on nanbeige.
4. gemma4a glitching & startup >60 s (mcp.json + warmup)
Solved

gemma4a exceeded 18.62 GiB under --cache-ram 4048 + Docker's 13 GiB VM. Cutting to --cache-ram 256, -c 49152, q4_0 KV stabilized it at 16.21 GiB (31.96 tok/s). Decoupling stdio Docker MCP cut cold swaps up to 8.3×.

Fix appliedSeparate tool vs stdio macros, --cache-ram 256/512, and -t 4 -tb 4 (4 P-cores).

Speculative decoding: math vs measured reality on Apple M4

Direct test of qwen3a embedded MTP + inkling2_loop capability check.

Why 80.2% acceptance still ran 2.59× slower

Speculative decoding only helps when verifying multiple tokens is cheaper than reading weights sequentially. On a memory-bandwidth-bound 93.2 GB/s M4 running a 35B MoE, qwen3a's embedded MTP draft layer (blk.40, 366 MB) hit 80.2% acceptance (mean length 1.80), yet throughput fell from 36.93 tok/s to 14.28 tok/s because verifying draft + target tokens activated the union of MoE experts and forced GDN recurrent state rollback across 41 layers (27.08 ms → 126.5 ms/step).

On Inkling-2-Mini-IQ4XS.gguf, llama-server build 12651 explicitly logs speculative decoding not supported by this context due to inkling2_loop's recurrent ShortConv + loop state.
