Environment diagnostics & fixes

nanb3 Metal OOM resolved 24 GB limit

Initial runs on nanb3 (4.17B params) failed with kIOGPUCommandBufferCallbackErrorOutOfMemory at sequence length 2,048 and stalled on trust_remote_code prompt. Adding --grad-checkpoint --max-seq-length 1024 --batch-size 1 --trust-remote-code stabilized training at ~4.7 GB RSS.

LFM3 & LFM3-VL text registration MLX native

Registered vendored lfm3.py for lfm3-1.7b and built lfm3_vl.py (stripping vision_tower and multi_modal_projector while preserving tie_word_embeddings: true) so lfm3-2b trains and serves cleanly in mlx_lm.

Excluded base models Incompatible

open-elm lacks tokenizer files in its directory and raises OpenELMConfig.__post_init__() got an unexpected keyword argument 'use_cache'. MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B (minicpmv4_7) is a VLM outside raw mlx_lm.lora.

Why Apple SFT drops steeply from ~3.0–3.7

The Apple dataset (9,139 samples) introduces domain formatting and response phrasing unfamiliar to base weights, yielding high initial cross-entropy (3.707 on Granite, 3.040 on LFM2-450M). Both models converged smoothly through step 600 without validation overfitting (val loss still falling at step 600), indicating additional epochs could lower loss further.

Why Software-coding plateaus near 0.57–0.60

Granite-4.0-H-350M already had strong code priors (step 1 val loss 0.899). Validation loss reached its minimum at step 400 (0.574) and edged up slightly to 0.612 / 0.595 by step 600. For coding evals, 0000400_adapters.safetensors is worth comparing against the final adapters.safetensors.

Why nanb3 throughput is ~51 tok/s vs 818 tok/s

nanb3 runs 4.17B effective unrolled parameters (num_loops=2, 22 layers) with grad_checkpoint=true (recomputing activations on backward pass) and grad_accumulation_steps=4. Despite running 16x slower, val loss halved from 0.702 to 0.316 by step 400; checkpoint 400 is usable as-is or resumable.

Antidoom FTPO analysis & elicited loops

Native MLX port of Liquid4All/antidoom Final Token Preference Optimization
L_FTPO = E[ clip((eps - delta_tok)/eps, 0, 1) * softplus(eps - delta_tok) ] + 0.4 * MSE_tether(V \ T) + 0.05 * MSE_excess(|diff_T| - tau)

How FTPO loss behaves statistically

Unlike full-sequence SFT, FTPO isolates only the single token position where a repetition loop begins (rejected_token_id) and pushes its logit below plausible alternatives sampled at that step (delta_tok = z_chosen - z_rejected, margin eps = 2.0). The 0.4 MSE tether anchors all other ~128k–166k vocabulary logits to the frozen base model so capabilities outside the loop boundary stay untouched.

Why async logprob deferral mattered

Our initial Antidoom script ran mx.argpartition + mx.eval on every generated token to record top-20 logprobs, stalling MLX's Metal pipeline. Deferring logprob extraction in antidoom_mlx_fast.py to a single forward pass at reject_idx only when find_inner_repetition fires eliminated per-token sync stalls and enabled 1536-token sampling.

Interpreting low loop yield (2–4 pairs)

With 15–22 minute caps per model on Apple Silicon, small and mid-size base models looped on ~1–5% of antidoom-mix-v1.0 prompts at temp=0.01. Training ran 4 epochs over those 2–4 pairs (4 optimizer steps, initial pref_loss 3.05–4.91). Notice mse_elem stayed tiny (0.0000–0.1032), confirming minimal drift from base weights; larger sample pools (50–200 pairs) require longer generation passes or relaxed thresholds (min_repeats=2).
